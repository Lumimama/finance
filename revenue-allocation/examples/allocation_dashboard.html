Sales invoiced implementation at $0 — a concession that looks free on paper. Relative-SSP allocation reassigns transaction price to it anyway, at exactly the contract's overall discount ratio. You can discount an invoice line; you cannot discount the accounting. The pro-rata column is a validation check, not a hope: any obligation whose ratio deviates from the contract's fails the build — a discount parked on one line is the classic manual-workaround error.

Conclusion: agent — net presentation. The arithmetic proof is check 5: profit is identical to the cent under either presentation ($0.13M), which is precisely why the control-of-the-good indicators — not the invoice total — make the call. Gross would have added $0.72M of pass-through to the revenue line. For a marketplace or reseller business this single judgment swings reported revenue by multiples.

Validation — re-run before every publish

  • PASS allocated obligations sum to transaction price, all 40 contracts — max gap $0.000000
  • PASS bundle discount allocates pro-rata (no obligation eats it alone) — max within-contract ratio spread 0.000001
  • PASS "free implementation": invoiced at $0, allocated revenue anyway — 14 contracts, $493K reassigned to implementation by relative SSP
  • PASS lifetime recognized revenue = transaction price, every contract — max gap $0.000000
  • PASS agent call: profit identical gross vs net; only revenue differs — gross would report $0.85M, net reports $0.13M — same $0.13M profit
  • PASS blended bundle discount within the stated band — 15.0%

SSP bases: platform and support are observable (standalone renewals and attach); implementation is cost-plus-margin; usage credits use an adjusted market assessment — the hierarchy stated, as a policy memo would.
