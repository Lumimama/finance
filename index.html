Payments

Payments Reconciliation Engine →

Revenue analytics

Usage-Based Revenue Engine →

AI pricing: license + metered usage against committed minimums. An ARR walk where usage expansion is its own line, a commit-utilization action list, and the rate-compression-vs-inference-cost curve.

● Live dashboard

Revenue Cohort Analysis →

Cohort heatmap, layer cake, and empirical CAC-payback curves by channel. Seeded with a promo cohort that dies at first renewal — invisible in blended NRR, unmissable in the heatmap.

● Live dashboard

Revenue Recognition →

Bookings → billings → revenue from a 900-contract ledger. Deferred and RPO roll-forwards tie to the cent every month; deferred unwind schedule and the RPO disclosure split.

● Live dashboard

Forward Revenue Model →

Next four quarters from the three books that exist today: sales capacity, stage-weighted pipeline with coverage flags, and a per-contract risk-weighted renewal book.

● Live dashboard

Pricing Waterfall →

List → pocket price across 1,100 deals. Quarter-end deals realize 5.9 points less — ~$810K given to the calendar — and discount depth creeps with deal size. The waterfall ties on every deal.

● Live dashboard

Variance Narrator →

Budget-vs-actuals in, board-ready variance commentary out. Python computes every figure; Claude drafts the narrative. The model never does arithmetic and never sees a number it wasn't handed.

Planning & cash

Integrated Three-Statement Model →

Income statement, balance sheet, and indirect cash flow, line by line across twelve quarters with scenario tabs. A = L + E to the cent in every column, or the model refuses to publish.

● Live dashboard

Monte Carlo Scenario Planner →

10,000 simulations of 12 quarters. P10/P50/P90 fans for ARR and cash, floor-breach probability, and a tornado ranking which assumption actually drives the outcome.

● Live dashboard

13-Week Cash Forecast →

Direct-method forecast from the AR aging, the AP aging, and the recurring commitments in neither. Low point, covenant floor test, and collections stress scenarios.

● Live dashboard

Cash Burn & Runway →

Gross vs net burn with the trailing 3-month line, runway trajectory, and the fundraise trigger drawn as a date on the chart. A seeded one-time prepay shows why single-month runway lies.

● Live dashboard

Reporting, metrics & controls

Board One-Pager →

Thirty metrics in five sections — growth, customer health, efficiency, financial, AI-specific — every one with its definition on the page, including GM after AI costs and cost per inference.

● Live dashboard

Board Metrics →

NRR, GRR, burn multiple, CAC payback, magic number, Rule of 40 — each computed by a function whose docstring states which definition it uses. The ARR walk has to tie, and the report says whether it did.

● Live dashboard

AI Infrastructure Metrics →

Cost per token and per inference, GPU utilization, the cost-latency frontier, revenue per token. A model-routing deploy cuts cost per call 41% at flat pricing — finance in an engineering decision.

● Live dashboard

Continuous Controls Monitor →

Seven audit detectors over a year of AP and T&E — duplicates, split invoices, Benford's law, policy and velocity anomalies. 100% recall against seeded ground truth, false-positive rate reported.
