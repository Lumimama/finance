finance

Finance tooling built for work that's already part of the job. Python, synthetic data, readable logic. Live dashboards below.

Payments Reconciliation Engine →

50,000 transactions matched against a bank settlement file. Every break classified — timing, amount, missing, duplicate, FX, fee — aged by dollar exposure and routed to an owner. Classification validated against seeded ground truth: every one of 1,148 breaks found and correctly labeled.

● Live dashboard · Python, no dependencies

Payments Unit Economics →

Contribution per transaction across 120,000 transactions — by rail, region, and merchant segment. Contribution waterfall, cross-border premium, and mix-shift sensitivity: what happens to blended economics when cross-border doubles or debit shifts to credit.

● Live dashboard · Python, no dependencies

Continuous Controls Monitor →

Seven audit detectors over a year of AP and T&E — duplicate payments, split invoices, Benford's law, round-dollar clustering, policy and velocity anomalies. Risk-scored review queue with 100% recall against seeded ground truth, and the false-positive rate reported instead of hidden.

● Live dashboard · Python, no dependencies

Board One-Pager →

Thirty metrics in five sections — growth, customer health, efficiency, financial performance, and AI-specific — every one with its definition on the page, including GM after AI costs and cost per inference. LTV:CAC is flagged as formulaic right on the scorecard.

● Live dashboard · Python, no dependencies

Cash Burn & Runway →

Gross vs net burn with the trailing 3-month line, runway trajectory (the slope, not the level), and the fundraise trigger drawn as a date on the chart. A one-time prepay is seeded to show why single-month runway lies and t3 is the quotable number.

● Live dashboard · Python, no dependencies

AI Infrastructure Metrics →

Cost per token and per inference, GPU utilization, the cost-latency frontier, and revenue per token. A model-routing deploy cuts cost per call 41% at flat pricing — finance participating in an engineering decision, quantified.

● Live dashboard · Python, no dependencies

Usage-Based Revenue Engine →

The finance layer of AI pricing: platform license + metered usage against committed minimums + implementation. An ARR walk with usage expansion as its own line, a commit-utilization action list (downgrade risks and the upsell list), and the rate-compression-vs-inference-cost curve every AI-native P&L lives or dies on.

● Live dashboard · Python, no dependencies

Revenue Cohort Analysis →

The cohort heatmap, the layer cake, and empirical CAC-payback curves by channel — 1,450 customers over 54 months. Seeded with a promo cohort that dies at first renewal: invisible in blended NRR, unmissable in the heatmap. That contrast is the argument for cohort analysis.

● Live dashboard · Python, no dependencies

Revenue Recognition →

Bookings → billings → revenue from a 900-contract ledger, with deferred revenue and RPO roll-forwards that tie to the cent every month, the deferred unwind schedule, and the RPO disclosure split. The three series everyone conflates, kept honest.

● Live dashboard · Python, no dependencies

Forward Revenue Model →

Next four quarters from the three books that exist today: sales capacity (reps × ramp × quota), stage-weighted pipeline with coverage flags, and a per-contract risk-weighted renewal book that names the largest at-risk accounts.

● Live dashboard · Python, no dependencies

Pricing Waterfall →

List → pocket price across 1,100 deals. Quarter-end deals realize 5.9 points less — ~$810K of price given to the calendar — and discount depth creeps with deal size beyond any cost-to-serve argument. The waterfall ties on every deal.

● Live dashboard · Python, no dependencies

13-Week Cash Forecast →

Direct-method forecast from the AR aging, the AP aging, and the recurring commitments in neither. Reports the low point and the week it lands, tests it against a covenant floor, and stress-tests runway against collections slipping.

● Live dashboard · Python, no dependencies

Board Metrics →

NRR, GRR, burn multiple, CAC payback, magic number, Rule of 40 — each computed by a function whose docstring states which definition it uses. The ARR walk has to tie, and the report says whether it did.

● Live dashboard · Python, no dependencies

Integrated Three-Statement Model →

Driver-based P&L → balance sheet → cash flow, quarterly over three years, three scenarios. Fully articulated — deferred revenue, SBC, working capital — and the balance sheet ties to the cent in every quarter or the model refuses to publish.

● Live dashboard · Python, no dependencies

Monte Carlo Scenario Planner →

10,000 simulations of 12 quarters with drivers drawn from distributions. P10/P50/P90 fans for ARR and cash, probability of breaching the board cash floor, and a tornado ranking which assumption actually drives the outcome — argue about the top two, the rest is noise management.

● Live dashboard · Python, no dependencies

Variance Narrator →

Budget-vs-actuals in, board-ready variance commentary out. Python computes every figure; Claude drafts the narrative from that computed evidence pack. The model never does arithmetic and never sees a number it wasn't handed.
