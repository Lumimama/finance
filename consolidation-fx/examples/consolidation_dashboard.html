CTA movement by month (UK + JP, USD)

Every bar is computed twice: as the balance-sheet plug and as the analytical roll-forward (opening net assets × spot move + NI × (spot − avg)). Validation fails if they diverge by a cent. A plug always balances — that is exactly why it cannot be the only computation.

US–JP2025-11 booked correctlyunder-accrued ¥27,000 ÷ 9 = 3,000 — transposition suspect

Japan accrued the November management fee with two digits transposed. A transposition error is always divisible by 9 — the oldest trick in the reconciler's book, and the matrix applies it automatically. The engine posted a top-side accrual in November and reversed it in December when Japan's own catch-up entry landed. Every other pair nets to zero in all twelve months.
