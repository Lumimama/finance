Management commentary — supplied by the preparer, not a calculated figure

July added 1.01 million dollars of new ARR against 668 thousand of expansion, so growth this month came from new logos rather than the installed base - the reverse of the June pattern. Nine new logos closed and three customers left. Ending ARR is 39.8 million dollars.

Gross retention softened. Churn of 232 thousand and contraction of 145 thousand together exceeded expansion, so the installed base contributed negatively before new logos are counted. The board should read the retention line as the weaker half of an otherwise strong month.

A 180 thousand dollar inference overage true-up from one customer was billed in July and is excluded from ARR. It is real revenue and appears in the revenue line; it is not a subscription commitment and would misstate both ARR and NRR if treated as expansion. Sales Operations classified it as an upsell in the CRM and the classification has been corrected for board reporting under FIN-POL-021 section 2.1. Commission and retention reporting owned by Sales Operations still treats the amount as expansion, and that difference is known, documented, and expected to persist until their next reporting cycle - it is a definitional difference, not an error in either system.

AI cost as a percentage of revenue continued to fall, which is the margin story the board asked to see tracked each month. Cost per thousand tokens benefited from a committed-use rebate received this month.

Runway is reported on trailing three-month burn. A single strong collections month would flatter the figure if it were computed on the latest month alone.
