Metric Definitions


The definitions rendered beside each metric on the dashboard are the approved definitions and are maintained in board.py, which is version-controlled. Where a metric has more than one defensible definition, this company uses:


NRR and GRR - chain-linked monthly on the installed base only; new logos are excluded from both numerator and denominator. GRR is capped at 100%.


Magic number - net new ARR for the quarter over the prior quarter's S&M. ARR is already an annual figure and is not annualized a second time.


CAC payback - all S&M over (new ARR x gross margin / 12). Deliberately conservative: it charges all sales and marketing to new logos.


Runway - cash over trailing three-month average net burn, not the single most recent month.


Gross margin - AI inference is fully loaded in COGS and is never adjusted out. The AI section reports the trend in AI cost as a percentage of revenue, because the direction of that line is the margin story.


LTV:CAC - formulaic (ARPA x GM / churn) and labeled as such on the page. The empirical cohort version is the one to cite externally.


Source Precedence


Where this document and the Google Sheet disagree on a number, the Sheet governs and the discrepancy is a data-entry defect to be corrected in the Sheet. Where this document and the dashboard disagree on a definition, this document governs and board.py is to be corrected. No figure is ever typed into this document.
