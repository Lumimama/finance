Metric Definitions


The definitions rendered beside each metric on the dashboard are the approved definitions and are maintained in board.py, which is version-controlled. Where a metric has more than one defensible definition, this company uses:


ARR - contracted recurring revenue only. Usage-based inference billed above the committed volume is revenue in the period earned and is never an ARR movement, because it carries no forward commitment. See Source Precedence for how the two are told apart.


NRR and GRR - chain-linked monthly on the installed base only; new logos are excluded from both numerator and denominator. GRR is capped at 100%.


Magic number - net new ARR for the quarter over the prior quarter's S&M. ARR is already an annual figure and is not annualized a second time.


CAC payback - all S&M over (new ARR x gross margin / 12). Deliberately conservative: it charges all sales and marketing to new logos.


Runway - cash over trailing three-month average net burn, not the single most recent month.


Gross margin - AI inference is fully loaded in COGS and is never adjusted out. The AI section reports the trend in AI cost as a percentage of revenue, because the direction of that line is the margin story.


LTV:CAC - formulaic (ARPA x GM / churn) and labeled as such on the page. The empirical cohort version is the one to cite externally.


Headcount - employees only. Contractors, agency workers and interns are excluded, so ARR per FTE is measured against payroll rather than against total workers.


Source Precedence


Where this document and the Google Sheet disagree on a number, the Sheet governs and the discrepancy is a data-entry defect to be corrected in the Sheet. Where this document and the dashboard disagree on a definition, this document governs and board.py is to be corrected. No figure is ever typed into this document.


Within the close package, the governing document is the Board Metrics Close Policy (FIN-POL-021). Where the CRM's movement_type conflicts with the billing system's revenue_basis, revenue_basis governs: it states what an amount is, while movement_type states how it arrived.


Management Commentary


July added 1.01 million dollars of new ARR against 668 thousand of expansion, so growth this month came from new logos rather than the installed base - the reverse of the June pattern. Nine new logos closed and three customers left. Ending ARR is 39.8 million dollars.


Gross retention softened. Churn of 232 thousand and contraction of 145 thousand together exceeded expansion, so the installed base contributed negatively before new logos are counted. The board should read the retention line as the weaker half of an otherwise strong month.


A 180 thousand dollar inference overage true-up from one customer was billed in July and is excluded from ARR. It is real revenue and appears in the revenue line; it is not a subscription commitment and would misstate both ARR and NRR if treated as expansion. Sales Operations classified it as an upsell in the CRM and the classification has been corrected for board reporting under FIN-POL-021 section 2.1. Commission and retention reporting owned by Sales Operations still treats the amount as expansion, and that difference is known, documented, and expected to persist until their next reporting cycle - it is a definitional difference, not an error in either system.


AI cost as a percentage of revenue continued to fall, which is the margin story the board asked to see tracked each month. Cost per thousand tokens benefited from a committed-use rebate received this month.


Runway is reported on trailing three-month burn. A single strong collections month would flatter the figure if it were computed on the latest month alone.
